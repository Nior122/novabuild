FAQs

Quick answers

How long does delivery take?
Port Harcourt: 1–2 business days. Lagos & Abuja: 2–3 business days. Other states: 3–5 business days. Express delivery is available at checkout.
Do you offer pay on delivery?
Yes, in Port Harcourt, Lagos and Abuja. Pay the rider in cash or by transfer when your order arrives. For other states, choose card or bank transfer at checkout.
What is your returns policy?
Return unused items in their original packaging within 7 days for a refund or exchange. If an item arrives faulty, we’ll cover the return delivery.
How does the warranty work?
Every product carries a 12-month warranty against manufacturing defects. Message us with your order number and we’ll repair or replace the item.
Which payment methods do you accept?
Debit and credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Verve), bank transfer, and pay on delivery in select cities.
